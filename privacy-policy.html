Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

KheleGames is a collection of casual and family-friendly games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the KheleGames Android application, including offline gameplay, optional online features, and support requests.

1. Information stored on your device

KheleGames may store game progress, scores, achievements, preferences, and gameplay settings locally on your device. This allows you to resume games and maintain progress. Local information may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Locally stored gameplay information is not necessarily transmitted to a server.

2. Feedback and support information

If you voluntarily submit feedback or contact us, we may receive your message, the game concerned, and information you choose to include.

When server-backed feedback is enabled, app version, submission time, and operating-system version may accompany reports to help investigate problems. Feedback may be queued locally when offline. A queued report is not considered delivered until the server confirms receipt.

Please do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.

3. Optional online multiplayer

If online Ludo or other multiplayer features are enabled, they may process player display names or pseudonymous identifiers, room codes, gameplay actions, connection status, and network information needed to connect players, synchronize games, prevent abuse, and restore interrupted sessions.

Online multiplayer is optional. Available offline games can be played without joining an online room.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to provide gameplay, maintain saved progress, operate optional online sessions, respond to feedback, investigate bugs, maintain security, and improve reliability.

5. Third-party services

When online features are enabled, limited information may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate those features. We do not intentionally publish private feedback messages or personal contact information.

Advertising, analytics, and purchase features may be introduced in future versions. Before enabling these services, this policy will be updated to disclose the providers, information processed, applicable choices, and data sharing.

6. Children's privacy

KheleGames is designed to be family-friendly. We encourage children to use online and feedback features with guidance from a parent or guardian. Children should not submit personal contact details or sensitive information.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review and address the request.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on the device until the app or its data is removed, subject to available reset controls.

Information received through feedback or online services is retained only as reasonably needed for support, troubleshooting, security, or applicable legal obligations.

You may request access to or deletion of information you provided by contacting us. We may need limited details to locate and verify the relevant record.

8. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
